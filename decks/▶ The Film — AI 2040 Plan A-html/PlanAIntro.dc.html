What is
Plan A?
AI companies are racing to build AIs that are smarter than humans in every way. In AI 2027, the authors predicted that race ends in extinction or an irreversible concentration of power.
Plan A is their positive vision of what should happen instead: an international deal with total research transparency, so many companies in many countries scale slowly and safely together.
Note well
A recommendation, not a prediction
I
Delay superintelligence until 2040
II
Make all AI research public
III
Let dozens of companies worldwide catch up to the frontier
IV
Enter Mutually Assured Compute Destruction
