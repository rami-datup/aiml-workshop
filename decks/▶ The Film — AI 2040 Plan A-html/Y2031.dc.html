The burden of proof flips: companies must now prove their AIs are safe in public “safety cases.”
Two lines of defense
Alignment
Give AIs the intended goals. Still out of reach: no AI reliably follows its spec.
Control
Limit what AIs can do even if they try: monitors from rival labs, security barriers, long delays before AI R&D use.
