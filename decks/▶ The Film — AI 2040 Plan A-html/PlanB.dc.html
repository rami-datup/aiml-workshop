How it plays out
1

Cyberwar escalates to physical sabotage: datacenters go dark, fab equipment is wrecked, and both sides prepare over Taiwan.

2030: the US automates AI R&D first. By 2031 the President must choose: handoff to superhuman AIs, or war.
