AI for epistemics
AI can help our ability to reach true beliefs (cheap, honest fact-checkers) or hurt it (cheap, dishonest astroturfing). The goal: reach the basin of sanity, where a sane society makes itself saner.
Basin of sanity
…and the opposite basin
Getting into the basin should be a top priority of the US government during AI takeoff.
Top interventions
1Let public figures prove they tell the truth via privacy-preserving audits and lie detection.
2Evaluate AIs’ epistemic virtue and reward labs whose AIs score well.
3Use AI in social and traditional media to improve discourse.
4Automated research assistants and forecasters.
5Help people past emotional blockers to good reasoning, rather than preying on them.
6Encourage adoption of these epistemic tools, and of AI generally.
