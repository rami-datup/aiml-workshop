Plan S — Shutdown
“Once men turned their thinking over to machines in the hope that this would set them free. But that only permitted other men with machines to enslave them.” — Frank Herbert, Dune
“We do not need to build god. We do not need to race China to build god. What we need is for nobody to build god.”
1
China agrees to a global moratorium: chips are tracked, large training runs and AI-algorithm research are banned, and existing AIs keep running.
2
By the end of 2030 the deal covers 99% of AI compute and 100% of advanced fabs. AI research becomes as taboo as human cloning.
3
Covert projects crawl at least 10× slower. Polished products built on frozen AI still deliver a sci-fi future.
