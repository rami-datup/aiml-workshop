The AI and robot economy
By 2035 AIs and robots can do 95% of all cognitive and physical tasks, so output tracks how many of them exist, and they can be built far faster than people grow up.
How fast the AI & robot population doubles
Plan A, capped by treaty~6 months
Unregulated, human-level AIweeks to months
Default world, no Plan A (2033)~1 month
A one-month doubling time means >1000× economic growth in a single year, maybe as robot fleets self-replicating in a desert. Shorter bar = faster doubling.
