Plan C — The Slowdown
“We must minimize the risks and maximize the benefits of AI.” — almost everyone in AI policy
How it plays out
1
The President promises strong regulation. By 2030 it’s clear AI R&D will be fully automated by year-end.
2
A deal fails: China insists on verifying US compliance, and Chinese inspectors in US datacenters are a nonstarter.
3
The leading company pauses for a few months. Then the companies push back: “20% of compute on safety, and we still beat China.”
4
Late 2030: the President sides with the companies and recursive self-improvement continues, as in AI 2027’s “Slowdown” ending.
