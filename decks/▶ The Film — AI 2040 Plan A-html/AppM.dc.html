Why does AI lead to explosive growth?
Today the size of the economy is tied to the human population. From 2032 it is tied to the population of AIs and robots, which grows far faster.
The arithmetic, US 2032
60M×20×2.5=3B
AI copies × speed × (longer hours, more effective) = human-equivalent AI workers
US cognitive labor
~100M
AI workers
30×
What AI & robots can do
Cognitive tasks50%
Physical tasks35%
Robots lag: 20M human-equivalent robots, 4× fewer than the US physical labor force.
So why not 30× growth?
Growth bottlenecks on what AI can’t yet do: the other half of cognitive tasks, physical work and capital.
But the bottlenecks only hold so long. The falling cost of GPUs and robots is dwarfed by their value, which drives massive investment.
