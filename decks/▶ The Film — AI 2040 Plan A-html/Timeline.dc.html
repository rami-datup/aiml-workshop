Overture · The timeline
Superintelligence, delayed from 2030 to 2040
Default path: AI R&D fully automated in 2030 → superintelligence by year-end
The pause · 2035–2040
2029
The US and China agree to avoid a reckless race to superintelligence.
2030
The default path would have reached superintelligence. Thanks to the deal, it doesn’t.
2030–35
Scale within the human range, up to AIs as capable as top human experts.
2035
Pause at top-human-expert AI to maintain human control.
2040
Unpause and scale to superintelligence. Hence the title.
