Reel III · The roads not taken
The Branch Point, 2029
After the 2028 election, the new President must choose how America answers superintelligence. The same starting point leads to five very different worlds.
A
The Deal
“International cooperation to avoid an imminent intelligence explosion.”
Leads to
Superintelligence in 2040, under shared, verified control
B
Sabotage
“A US-led coalition to govern AI development.”
Leads to
Cyberwar and sabotage; by 2031, handoff or war
C
The Slowdown
“Strong regulation to ensure safety and security.”
Leads to
A few months’ pause, then the race resumes (AI 2027 “Slowdown”)
D
The Race
“Light-touch regulation to prioritize AI innovation.”
Leads to
Superintelligence by early 2031 (AI 2027 “Race”)
S
Shutdown
“A global moratorium on AI development.”
Leads to
AI frozen near 2029 levels, likely for years, probably not forever
